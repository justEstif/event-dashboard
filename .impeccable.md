# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Sports fans and event-goers who browse and manage sporting events. They use the app across both **mobile and desktop** — mobile context is common (quick lookups, on-the-go), so touch targets, readable type, and responsive layouts are essential. They're engaged and enthusiastic about sport, not power users of enterprise tooling.

### Brand Personality
**Energetic · Sporty · Bold**

The interface should feel alive — like a sports broadcast or a match-day programme. It should evoke excitement and confidence, not corporate calm. Users should feel like they're in the action, not filling out a form.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Style**: Bold and sporty — strong typography, confident use of color, punchy cards. Not minimal-utilitarian, not generic SaaS.
- **Primary color**: Vivid sport red — a saturated, high-energy red (approx. `hsl(0 85% 50%)`) in the tradition of ESPN, Formula 1, and major league sports brands. Avoid muddy or dark maroon tones. Keep it bright and confident.
- **Anti-references**: Generic enterprise dashboards (grey, flat, lifeless). Avoid the "default shadcn" look — neutrals everywhere, no personality.
- **References**: ESPN, Sofascore, Formula 1 website — bold color blocks, strong typographic hierarchy, sport-specific color coding.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode supported. Dark mode should feel premium and immersive (deep neutral backgrounds, red accents pop).

### Design Principles
1. **Energy first** — every screen should feel dynamic. Use bold headings, vivid color, and confident spacing. Avoid timid neutrals as the dominant visual tone.
2. **Sport-native** — the sport badge color system is a feature, not decoration. Lean into it. Colors should be saturated and distinct.
3. **Mobile-ready by default** — touch targets minimum 44px, type readable at arm's length, cards stack naturally. Design mobile-first, enhance for desktop.
4. **Hierarchy through contrast** — use size, weight, and color to create clear visual priority. The event name, sport, and date should be scannable in under a second.
5. **Red is intentional** — primary red is reserved for brand moments and CTAs (buttons, active states, highlights). It is never used for errors/destructive actions, which use a distinct destructive red variant.
